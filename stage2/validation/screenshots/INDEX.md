The planned collection is complete: 12 images covering one buried and one exposed anchor per protein, each from two orientations. The views use surface, cartoon, and highlighted sticks, with varying amounts of protein context. They support visual inspection but do not independently establish numerical SASA or residue identity. Identity follows user identification and prepared-session context; labels are not readable. Transparent surfaces can reveal enclosed atoms. These limitations are retained rather than treating the images as independent proof of classification.
